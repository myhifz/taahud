Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

No data collected · no account · no server · no ads · no tracking

In short

Ta'ahud does not collect any data. There is no account, no server, no advertising and no tracking or analytics tool.

What stays on your phone

Your first name, your programme, the surahs you tick, the surahs to strengthen, your new pages, your goal, your profile photo and your settings are stored only inside the app, on your device. Nobody else can access them, not even the app's author.

Phone backup

If iCloud (iPhone) or Google (Android) backup is turned on, this data is included in your phone's backup, like your other apps. It is managed by Apple or Google under their own terms.

Permissions

Photos: only if you choose a profile photo. Only that photo is used; it is resized and kept on your phone.

Notifications: only if you turn on the reminder. Reminders are scheduled by the phone itself; nothing goes over the internet.

Deleting your data

In the app: Settings › Danger zone › "Reset all data". Or simply delete the app.

Children

Ta'ahud can be used at any age. Since no data is collected, no children's data is collected either.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page and its date will be updated. Contact: taahud.app@gmail.com
